# Message Reminders Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Message Reminders is made by Faizi Fifita. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: it collects nothing. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

## What we collect

Nothing. Message Reminders has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking. The app does not connect to the internet, and we never receive any of your data.

## What stays on your device

The messages, schedules, and lists you create are stored only on your iPhone. They are removed
when you delete them or delete the app.

## Contacts

When you choose who a message is for, you pick them using Apple's contact picker. The app saves
that contact's name, the phone number or email you picked, and their photo alongside your
message, on your device.

If you allow Contacts access, the app also reads a chosen contact's birthday to schedule birthday
messages. If you enter a birthday in the app, it saves that date to the contact's card in your
Contacts. The app does not read, copy, or upload the rest of your address book.

## Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

## Messages and clipboard

When you tap Compose, the app opens Apple's Messages with your text filled in. Nothing is sent
until you tap Send yourself. For a contact without a phone number or email, the app copies your
message to the clipboard instead, so you can paste it into another app.

## Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your information to anyone, because we never have it.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
